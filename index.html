About Brain Explorer

My goal with Brain Explorer is to make the brain a dynamic 3D canvas you can paint on and explore. You can see where a part lives, watch how chemical messengers bathe its circuits, and zoom from whole regions down to firing cells.

If you spot a bug or have feedback, I would love to hear from you.

Connect your AI key

Brain Explorer runs serverless in your browser. Connect your own Azure OpenAI or OpenAI API key to ask questions and paint neural journeys in 3D.

🔒 Your key is saved only in this browser's localStorage. It is sent directly to the AI provider and never touches any proxy or server.
