## 3. High-Risk Scientific Priority 1 (Batch A): Resolved in E2

### Pathway: stress-and-fear
- Primary Source: Stockhorst & Antov (2015), Frontiers in Behavioral Neuroscience (10.3389/fnbeh.2015.00359).
- Corrections Implemented in E2:
  1. Separated rapid sympathetic adrenaline mobilization (seconds) from slower endocrine cortisol release along the HPA axis (minutes). Replaced "chemical flood" phrasing with measured physiological descriptions.
  2. Removed false claim that prefrontal cortex literally "goes offline". Replaced with accurate description of cognitive shifts toward habitual and reflex-driven control during high stress.
  3. Eliminated acute vs chronic conflation: clarified that dendritic spine retraction occurs over weeks of prolonged chronic stress, whereas acute stress primarily alters immediate synaptic signaling.
  4. Resolved the projection contradiction: Step 4 and Step 5 now consistently describe prefrontal glutamatergic excitatory projections recruiting inhibitory intercalated cells in the amygdala.
  5. Framed rodent infralimbic circuitry accurately, noting corresponding ventromedial and medial prefrontal regions in humans.
- Dependent Surfaces Reconciled in E2:
  - `src/content/chemicals/hormones.js`: In `cortisol`, updated GR receptor text to remove deterministic claims of extinction blockage, and updated breaks bullet 3 to describe shifted contextual balance rather than permanent trauma.
  - `src/content/cells/inhibitory.js`: In `itc`, replaced "panic switch", "clamping shut", and "permanent trauma" metaphors with accurate physiological gating and fear renewal science.
  - `src/content/diagrams/index.js`: In `fear-circuit`, updated caption and relabeled node `pfc` to "Medial PFC" with rodent infralimbic context.
  - `src/content/glossary/index.js`: Added missing terms `extinction` and `stress`. Updated `infralimbic cortex`, `intercalated cells`, and `extinction learning` to reflect human/rodent context and non-erasing safety memory mechanisms.
  - `src/content/ask-presets.js`: Updated preset label from "stress locks in fear and trauma" to "stress impairs safety learning".

### Pathway: smelling
- Primary Source: Courtiol & Wilson (2017), PMC5362339.
- Corrections Implemented in E2:
  1. Replaced universal thalamic bypass claims ("the only sense that skips the thalamic switchboard") with accurate statement that smell reaches primary olfactory cortex without an initial thalamic relay, while noting downstream mediodorsal thalamus recruitment.
  2. Corrected hippocampal connectivity: replaced the claim of direct bulb-to-hippocampus axons with the true anatomical path (bulb projects to primary olfactory cortex and cortical amygdala; hippocampal access occurs through adjacent entorhinal and parahippocampal gateways).
  3. Reconciled 3D scene route: updated Step 2 route arc to `[['olfactory-bulb', 'amygdala'], ['amygdala', 'hippocampus']]` with explicit multi-hop route notes in evidence registry.
  4. Added mediodorsal thalamus focus and mention in Step 3 flavor and recognition processing.
- Dependent Surfaces Reconciled in E2:

## 4. High-Risk Scientific Priority 2 (Batch B): Resolved in E2

### Pathway: dopamine-pathways
- Primary Sources:
  - Watabe-Uchida et al. (2017), PMC6721851 (Neural circuitry of reward prediction error).
  - Gao et al. (2024), PMC10915723 (Dopamine and opioid receptor antagonism in human reward processing).
- Corrections Implemented in E2:
  1. Preserved the crucial wanting versus liking distinction, while clarifying that reward prediction error describes specific recorded populations in animal models rather than a universal law across all dopamine cells.
  2. Corrected expected reward step: clarified that lack of an extra burst does not mean dopamine stops operating or that subjective pleasure ceases.
  3. Corrected disappointment step: clarified that a transient dip in firing is a neural error signal, not identical to the subjective conscious feeling of disappointment.
  4. Refined movement step: framed dopamine action as tuning competing striatal pathways through the globus pallidus and thalamus, rather than acting as a single on/off brake switch.
  5. Refined habit step: removed deterministic timetable claims ("every day at eight o'clock for months") in favor of contextual habit formation dynamics.
- Dependent Surfaces Reconciled in E2:

  - `src/content/glossary/index.js`: Updated `reward prediction error` definition to emphasize teaching and motivational signaling across examined populations.

### Pathway: pain
- Primary Source: Cortes-Altamirano et al. (2018), PMC5883380 (5-HT receptors and modulation of pain response).
- Corrections Implemented in E2:
  1. Replaced split-stream narrative with integrated multidimensional pain science: nociceptive input is shaped across sensory-discriminative and affective-motivational networks that interact continuously.
  2. Corrected descending modulation: replaced the claim that descending serotonin acts as a uniform pain brake with the scientific consensus that descending serotonergic modulation is bidirectional, producing antinociception (via 5-HT1) or pronociceptive facilitation (via 5-HT2/3) depending on receptor subtypes and context.
- Dependent Surfaces Reconciled in E2:
  - `src/content/chemicals/modulators.js`: In `serotonin`, updated descending raphe projection tract (`caudal-raphe`) description and job to reflect bidirectional modulation of nociception.

### 1. remembering

- Corrections Implemented in E3:

## 8. Guardrails for Generated Answers (Packet E5 Complete)

The AI generation and export boundaries have been reinforced to ensure generated narratives remain strictly separated from verified, checked content:
1. **Synchronized System Prompts (`src/services/chat.js` and `server.py`)**:
   - Both client-side and server-side prompts share identical scientific boundary rules (rules 11 through 15).
   - Rule 11 (No Invented Sources): Forbids fabricating citations, paper titles, author names, or URLs. The model operates as an educational narrator without internet access or fake literature attribution.
   - Rule 12 (Direct vs. Indirect Pathways): Mandates distinguishing direct axonal projections from multi-step or relay circuits.
   - Rule 13 (Species Differences): Distinguishes animal models (rodents) from human brain circuitry.
   - Rule 14 (Receptor and Context Dependence): Qualifies modulatory transmitters as receptor-dependent rather than universal switches.
   - Rule 15 (No Deterministic Claims): Avoids deterministic or diagnostic medical claims.
   - Removed raw em dash character from server prompt rules.
2. **Visible Disclaimer in Ask UI (`src/ui/ask.js` and `styles.css`)**:
   - Added `.chat-warning-tag` rendering "AI draft &middot; May contain mistakes" directly on `.chat-card-meta` next to "Generated answer".
   - Warning is visible immediately without relying on mouse hover or tooltips, and wraps responsively on mobile screens.
3. **Unchecked Pathway Export Boundary (`src/ui/ask.js`)**:
   - Updated `scriptToPathwayJs`:
     - Ensures stable kebab-case IDs for each generated step.
     - Prepends `// Status: draft (unreviewed AI generation)` and explicit guidance that inclusion in curated pathways requires external human review, evidence mapping, and source verification.
     - Never outputs `checked: true`, source metadata, or SHA-256 fingerprints, ensuring generated drafts cannot satisfy the checked-content gate.
